# Incumbent SEO and marketing platforms: AI search visibility offerings (as of 13 October 2026)

Retrieval note: vendor pricing and product pages were fetched on 13 Oct 2026 unless a different date is given. Items dated before 2026 are marked "(pre-2026, possibly outdated)". Many third-party "reviews" in this space are written by competing AI-visibility vendors (Profound, Analyze AI, Dageno, Mentionable, LLM Pulse, Trakkr and others), so their framing is flagged where used. Where sources conflict, both versions are given.

## Q1. Semrush (AI Visibility Toolkit, Semrush One, Enterprise AIO) and its new owner Adobe (LLM Optimizer, now Adobe Brand Visibility)

### Takeaway
Semrush now sells AI visibility at three levels: a $99/month standalone AI Visibility Toolkit (25 tracked prompts, one domain, per-user licences), Semrush One bundles at $199 to $549/month (50 to 200 tracked prompts plus the SEO toolkit), and custom-priced Enterprise AIO. All three run on a prompt database Semrush says holds 317M+ prompts and responses, collected "not via any APIs." Since Adobe closed its acquisition of Semrush on 28 Apr 2026, Semrush's AI data also powers Adobe Brand Visibility (formerly LLM Optimizer), an enterprise product with no public price.

- Adobe completed its acquisition of Semrush on 28 April 2026 at $12.00 per share in cash (about $1.9B). Semrush became a wholly owned subsidiary and was delisted from the NYSE (ticker SEMR). The deal had been announced on 19 Nov 2025. — [StockTitan (8-K reprint)](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/SEMR/8-k-se-mrush-holdings-inc-reports-material-event-ffb33e197e44.html); [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/adobe-completes-semrush-acquisition-ai-search-visibility). ### Inferences
- Semrush has built a clear upgrade path: free checker (3 uses a day), then Free plan, then the $99 toolkit, then Semrush One at $199 to $549, then Enterprise AIO and Adobe Brand Visibility. AI visibility is now embedded at every rung, including the $139 SEO plan. A standalone tool selling basic "brand mention tracking" competes with something many Semrush SEO customers already have.
- Semrush's main prompt database excludes Perplexity; Perplexity appears only in the weekly Brand Performance report. That leaves room for a Perplexity-first keyword report like AISEO's. Ahrefs, SE Visible, Similarweb and HubSpot do track Perplexity (see Q2 and Q3), so this is not a unique gap across incumbents.
- At $99 per user with no shared limits, adding seats to the standalone toolkit gets expensive quickly. Agencies and teams are pushed toward Semrush One or Enterprise.
- The Adobe ownership is pulling Semrush's AI data into enterprise contracts. One agency reviewer warns that past Adobe acquisitions changed pricing within 12 to 24 months (see Q5). Pricing of Semrush's self-serve AI tiers could change, but there is no evidence yet that it has.

### Gaps
- Semrush's knowledge-base pages disagree on the AI Visibility Toolkit's platform list, and on whether Gemini is in Prompt Tracking. The official Enterprise AIO engine list could not be retrieved (the Adobe-hosted page returned 503).
- The SEO plan's AI prompt allowance, and whether the Free plan includes prompt tracking, are not stated on the pricing page.
- The general-availability date and pricing of Adobe Brand Visibility could not be confirmed from Adobe.
- Semrush does not say whether collection is logged-in or logged-out, or how many times each prompt is sampled.

## Q2. Ahrefs (Brand Radar AI and related features)

### Takeaway
Ahrefs Brand Radar puts a very large pre-built "AI Visibility Index" (about 476M monthly prompts across six engines, mostly AI Overviews) alongside metered custom prompt tracking. Ahrefs says every index prompt is "modeled from real user searches in Ahrefs' keyword database." The index costs from $199/month for selected platforms to $699/month for all of them. Custom prompts are bundled free in small amounts on paid plans, with packs from $50/month. Reviewers question how accurate the ChatGPT and Perplexity numbers are and describe the product as snapshot- and keyword-first.

### Cited Findings
- **What Brand Radar is:** It tracks brand visibility across AI answers, YouTube and Reddit. Its modules are Custom Prompts, AI Visibility (the AI Visibility Index), AI Analytics and AI Sources. — [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Index size and platforms:**
  - Index: "475M+ total monthly prompts" — [Brand Radar page](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar); "476M+ organic prompts" — [Ahrefs pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)
  - Per-platform prompt counts: AI Overviews 269,066,840; AI Mode 91,002,465; Copilot 29,198,545; Gemini 29,091,378; Perplexity 29,064,822; ChatGPT 28,522,328.
  - Claude is available for custom prompts, and each Claude update uses 8 checks.
  - Source: [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **How prompts are generated:**
  - Index: "Every prompt is modeled from real user searches in Ahrefs' keyword database."
  - Custom prompts: these can be entered by hand or AI-generated from competitor comparisons, pricing data and other signals, with location, language and focus-area settings.
  - Source: [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
  - The free checker page calls them "search-backed prompts... rather than synthetic ones." — [Ahrefs AI Visibility Checker](https://ahrefs.com/ai-visibility-checker)
- **Refresh:** Custom prompts can be tracked daily, weekly or monthly. Plan checks refresh daily, and the index has a "custom refresh cadence." — [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Pricing (Ahrefs' own pages, 13 Oct 2026):**
  - Pricing page: "Brand Radar AI from $199/mo." — [Ahrefs pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)
  - Free checker page: "$199/month for selected platforms and $699/month for all platforms, the latter including 2,500 custom prompt checks per month." — [Ahrefs AI Visibility Checker](https://ahrefs.com/ai-visibility-checker)
  - Brand Radar page, rendered ambiguously: AI Visibility Index at $199/mo "includes 83 prompts/day, 2,500+ checks/month, all platforms," shown next to a "$699/MO FOR ALL MODELS" label. — [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Custom prompt packs:** One "check" is one prompt on one platform in one location for one update.
  - Basic: $50/month for 2,500 checks, $0.020 per extra check.
  - Growth: $100/month for 7,000 checks, $0.015 per extra check.
  - Scale: $250/month for 25,000 checks, $0.010 per extra check.
  - Source: [Ahrefs pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)
- **Free allowance on paid plans:**
  - Custom prompts are free on any paid plan from Lite up. Lite gets 150 checks/month (5 prompts daily), Standard 300 (10), Advanced 600 (20), and Enterprise from 2,500 (from 83).
  - Index overage is $0.020 per check.
  - Source: [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Base plans (monthly):** Starter $29 (shown as a trial plan), Lite $129, Standard $249, Advanced $449, Enterprise $1,499 with an annual commitment. Annual billing saves "up to 17%." — [Ahrefs pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing)
- **Other modules:**
  - AI Sources tracks YouTube (titles, descriptions, transcripts), TikTok trends and Reddit; these are free to Ahrefs customers while in beta.
  - AI Analytics covers AI referral traffic (free with Web Analytics) and AI bot visits (Bot Analytics, free in beta).
  - Source: [Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar)
- **Third-party cost estimates:** A realistic floor of about $828/month once the Lite plan is added ($129 + $699). — [Nbound review](https://nboundmarketing.com/research/ai-visibility/ahrefs-brand-radar/); [Analyze AI review (competitor)](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/blog/ahrefs-brand-radar-review). An agency put six-platform coverage at $828 to $1,148/month on top of base plans (May 2026). — [Predicta Digital](https://predictadigital.com.au/blog/ahrefs-vs-semrush-vs-se-ranking-ai-visibility/)
- **Accuracy criticism (competitor-authored, so possibly biased):**
  - Brand Radar uses "a static prompt library and timed snapshots rather than... live, prompt-level executions." — [Profound review](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ahrefs-brand-radar-review)
  - A Dageno test (April 2026) found Brand Radar reporting 3 ChatGPT mentions for a brand that had 123 when counted manually. This is cited by an agency reviewer and is anecdotal. — [Predicta Digital](https://predictadigital.com.au/blog/ahrefs-vs-semrush-vs-se-ranking-ai-visibility/); [Dageno review](https://www.dageno.ai/blog/ahrefs-brand-radar-review)
  - Agency reviewer's verdict: Brand Radar is best for strategic research, not day-to-day client reporting or agencies running AI tracking for more than 2 or 3 clients. — [Predicta Digital](https://predictadigital.com.au/blog/ahrefs-vs-semrush-vs-se-ranking-ai-visibility/)

### Inferences
- Ahrefs' metered price of $0.010 to $0.020 per prompt-platform-location run gives a market reference for the cost of a raw AI answer. AISEO Pro ($29 for 25 scans × 5 prompts × 2 engines = 250 answers, plus 100 keyword reports) cannot win on raw answer volume. Its value has to come from the analysis layer: source mix, competing brands, prompt ideas, clusters and Claude-written takeaways.
- Ahrefs' index is heavily weighted toward Google surfaces: about 360M of the ~476M prompts are AI Overviews and AI Mode. Its ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity samples (about 29M each) are prompts modelled from search keywords, not observed chat logs. A tool that shows live answers to the user's own prompts can position itself as "what the engine actually says today" against "index estimates."
- Bundling free custom prompts into every paid plan (5 prompts daily on Lite) means Ahrefs subscribers have basic tracking at no extra cost. A standalone tool needs a reason to exist beyond that.

### Gaps
- Ahrefs' methodology page was not opened. A secondary review says index prompts are seeded from Google People Also Ask plus the keyword database, expanded with query fan-out, and run through each platform's public web interface. This is unverified.
- The exact mapping of $199 ("selected platforms" or per platform) against $699 ("all platforms") differs across Ahrefs' own pages. Regional (EUR) pricing was not verified.
- No independent, controlled accuracy benchmark was found.

## Q3. Other incumbents: SE Ranking (SE Visible), Similarweb, Moz, Conductor, BrightEdge, Search Atlas, Writesonic, Surfer, Yoast, Wix, HubSpot AEO, Rank Math, Ubersuggest, Botify, seoClarity

### Takeaway
Nearly every SEO suite, CMS and marketing platform now has an AI visibility module. Self-serve entry prices cluster around $79 to $129/month: SE Visible $99, Similarweb $99 annual / $129 monthly, Surfer $82 annual, Writesonic $79 annual. HubSpot ($50) and Yoast (about $30/month on annual billing) sit lower, and Wix includes it at no extra cost. Enterprise suites (Conductor, BrightEdge, Adobe) are quote-only. Data methods differ: SE Visible and Semrush capture answers from the engines' web interfaces, while Conductor explicitly uses official APIs.

## Q4. Free tools that compete with paid entry tiers (HubSpot AI Search Grader and other free checkers)

### Takeaway
Large incumbents now give away credible one-off AI visibility snapshots without signup: HubSpot's grader, Semrush's checker (3 runs a day), Ahrefs' checker (top-5 preview), plus Backlinko's 150-query checker. Microsoft gives verified sites first-party Copilot citation data for free. These free tools set the floor for what a free tier must beat. They mostly withhold the actual prompts and answers, history, and competitor depth.

## Q5. Bundling AI visibility with classic SEO data: distribution advantages and reviewer views on quality versus specialists

### Takeaway
Incumbents use three levers that standalone tools lack:
- **Installed base and bundling:** AI tracking is included in plans that customers already pay for.
- **Adjacent data:** search volume, keywords, backlinks and traffic panels are used to size and seed prompts.

Reviewers generally say the incumbent features are convenient for reporting and "directional," but question their accuracy and describe them as keyword-first and snapshot-based. Independent benchmarks are essentially absent.

### Inferences
- **Commoditisation:** Basic mention and citation tracking is becoming a free add-on inside SEO subscriptions that buyers already hold. For a standalone tool, "we track your brand in ChatGPT" is no longer a reason to buy for anyone with an Ahrefs, Semrush, SE Ranking, Moz, Wix or HubSpot plan. Specialists have to win on things bundles do poorly:
  - transparency: real prompts and full live answers;
  - analysis quality: why competitors win, and what to change;
  - engines the incumbents underweight: Perplexity is missing from Semrush's prompt database, and Claude is rare outside Conductor, Ahrefs custom prompts and Yoast;
  - price for small users who do not want a $99 to $199 suite.
- **AISEO's opening:** AISEO's $29 and $79 tiers target the segment incumbents serve least: small businesses and freelancers who do not hold an SEO suite. Its nearest incumbent rivals there are HubSpot AEO ($50, 3 engines, daily), Yoast AI+ (about $30, weekly, WordPress only) and Wix (free, Wix sites only). The free checkers from Semrush and Ahrefs are the main threat to conversion from AISEO's free tier.
- **Volume data:** Incumbents attach search volume and difficulty to prompts (Semrush, Moz, Adobe). AISEO's keyword reports do not appear to. Without volume or demand signals, AISEO's prompt ideas and clusters may look less decision-ready next to Semrush or Moz prompt research.
- **Variance and scan design:** SE Ranking reports that AI Mode repeats results only 9.2% of the time, and SE Visible says only about a third of cited domains repeat across runs. A 5-prompt, single-run scan is therefore highly sensitive to sampling noise. Incumbents answer this with daily repeated tracking and large prompt sets. AISEO should disclose variance or add repeat sampling to stay credible against reviewers' "directional only" framing.
- **Many brands will score zero:** The SEJ finding (most brands have no AI mentions) suggests many AISEO scan users will see zero mentions. That makes actionable "who is cited instead, and why" output more valuable than a score.

### Gaps
- No independent, controlled accuracy benchmark comparing incumbent AI visibility data with specialist tools or ground truth was found. Most quality judgments come from agencies or competing vendors.
- No first-party data was found on adoption or usage of incumbents' AI modules (for example, how many Semrush One or HubSpot AEO subscribers there are) beyond HubSpot's 850+ beta participants.
- Search Engine Land and Search Engine Journal articles could not be fetched (403), so their editorial reviews of incumbent tools are not represented beyond search snippets.
