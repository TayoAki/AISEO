# Self-serve, SMB-priced AI search visibility trackers (as of 13 October 2026)

Conventions used throughout these notes:
- "Fetched 13 Oct 2026" means I read the vendor's live pricing page on that date. These are the most current data points. Third-party dates are given inline. Anything dated before 2026 is flagged **[pre-2026, possibly outdated]**.
- **Price per tracked prompt** = monthly list price ÷ prompts included (monthly billing unless noted).
- **Price per answer** = monthly price ÷ (prompts × engines included × runs per month). Runs per month: daily = 30, every 2 days = 15, twice weekly ≈ 8.67, weekly ≈ 4.33. All per-prompt and per-answer figures marked "(calc)" are my arithmetic from the cited inputs, not vendor figures. They are list-price maximums: they assume the plan is fully used.
- Vendor-written comparison articles (LLM Pulse, Ranketta, Trakkr, Ryze, SE Visible, Rework, Rankability, Surfer) have commercial bias. They are cited for prices they checked, not for their verdicts.

## 1. Which self-serve tools matter as of October 2026?

### Takeaway
The self-serve tier under $500/month has consolidated around a handful of named vendors. **Peec AI** is the funded leader (about $29M raised, more than $10M ARR by May 2026). **Otterly.AI** has the lowest entry price and the largest user base (40,000+ users claimed, self-funded). **Rankscale**, **Trakkr**, **LLMrefs**, **Knowatoa**, **Promptmonitor**, **AI Peekaboo**, **Geoptie** and **Am I On AI** fill the $29 to $500 band. **Hall has effectively left the self-serve market**: Tracksuit acquired it in July 2026, and usehall.com now shows only a waitlist. Second-tier names that keep appearing in 2026 roundups are LLM Pulse, ZipTie, Keyword.com's AI Tracker, Rankshift, First Answer and Ranketta.

## 2. Plans, limits, free tiers and effective price per prompt and per answer

### Takeaway
List prices cluster at **$1 to $2 per tracked prompt per month** on entry plans.
- Low outliers: LLMrefs at about $0.16 per generated prompt with weekly refresh, Promptmonitor Growth at $0.78, and Am I On AI at $0.67.
- High outliers: Geoptie Starter at $3.27, and Otterly Lite at $5.07 once all 7 engines are added.

Per collected answer, daily-tracking plans range from about **$0.004 (Trakkr Scale) to $0.027 (Geoptie Starter, monthly)**. Most entry plans fall at **$0.013 to $0.021 per answer**. The outliers are Am I On AI (about $0.077 if weekly) and Claude answers on Rankscale ($0.165).

Packaging varies widely:
- Peec and Otterly sell a fixed number of engines and charge for more (Claude is the most expensive add-on).
- Trakkr, LLMrefs and Promptmonitor bundle all engines.
- Rankscale sells credits with engine-weighted costs (Claude costs 8× ChatGPT).
- AI Peekaboo moved in September 2026 to a slider configured by brands × prompts × models × cadence.

True free tiers are rare. Most vendors offer 7- or 14-day trials (some need a card) plus a free one-off "visibility report".

### Inferences
- The market anchors entry pricing at **$29/mo**: Otterly Lite and Promptmonitor Starter both sit there. That is exactly AISEO Pro's price point.
- At $29, competitors deliver 1,800 to 2,250 recurring answers a month, against AISEO Pro's 550 one-off answers. On raw answer volume, AISEO is 3 to 6 times more expensive per answer. AISEO's differentiation therefore has to rest on analysis (Claude-written keyword research, source mix, prompt ideas, clusters), not on monitoring volume.
- Per-answer list prices fall steeply with tier: roughly $0.02 at entry to $0.004 to $0.01 at higher tiers. Bright Data-style collection costs, which AISEO records in `report_usage`, should be benchmarked against the $0.004 to $0.02 retail band to see how much margin the competitors could have.
- "Per prompt" pricing hides huge differences in cadence and engine count. Weekly tools (LLMrefs, LLM Pulse, Am I On AI) look 3 to 10 times cheaper per prompt than daily tools but collect far fewer answers.
- Claude is consistently the priciest engine to add: Otterly's Claude add-on costs 3 to 4 times its Gemini and AI Mode add-ons, Rankscale charges 2 credits against 0.25 for ChatGPT, Peec keeps Claude Enterprise-only on brand plans, and Peekaboo charges a "premium". This suggests vendors pay more to collect Claude answers, likely through the API with web search, or see lower demand for it.

**API vs UI divergence**
- A Surfer study (data collected 4 Aug 2026) ran 1,000 prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Mode and AI Overviews, collecting 13,779 answers via both UI scraping and API, with one sample per prompt. Findings:
  - Brand overlap between UI and API answers was only 21 to 32% after canonicalization.
  - ChatGPT's API named 13.8 brands per answer against 7.9 in the UI.
  - ChatGPT's UI cited 12.1 sources against 3.1 via the API.
  - Domain-level source overlap ranged from 4.8% (ChatGPT) to 26.7% (Perplexity).

### Inferences
- UI-based collection is now table stakes in marketing copy. "API vs UI" is a live buyer objection, and AISEO can truthfully claim live consumer-UI answers (via Bright Data) for Perplexity, ChatGPT and Gemini.
- The cheapest per-answer tools (Trakkr Scale at $0.004, Geoptie Pro at $0.008) do not disclose their method. Prices that low may imply API collection or sparse sampling, but this is unverified.
- Vendors almost never state runs per prompt per day beyond "daily". No tool in this set advertises multiple samples per prompt per run.

### Gaps
- None of the vendors disclosed whether they use third-party scraping or data providers (Bright Data, DataForSEO, SerpApi, Oxylabs, cloro).
- No method statements were found for Trakkr, Geoptie, Knowatoa, LLMrefs or Am I On AI.
- No vendor states the logged-in vs logged-out state except Peec (logged-out).
